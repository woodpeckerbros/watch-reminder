Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Watch Reminder handles information when you use the App on your Wear OS watch and any paired companion device.

Information We Do Not Collect

Watch Reminder does not collect, transmit, store, sell, rent, share, or otherwise provide personal information to the developer or third parties.

Information Processed on Your Devices

The App processes reminders, settings, snooze settings, backup data, location information for religious times, and device state information locally on your devices.

Location Data

The App may access location information to calculate location-based religious times and reminders. Location information remains on your devices and is not transmitted to the developer.

Sensor and Activity Information

The App may access sensor information, activity recognition data, sleep-state indicators, and watch-worn status solely to suppress notifications while sleeping, avoid unnecessary notifications, and reduce battery consumption. The App is not intended for medical use.

Synchronization and Backup

The App may synchronize reminders and settings between a Wear OS watch and a paired phone using local device-to-device communication mechanisms such as Bluetooth.

Advertising and Analytics

The App does not display advertisements and does not use advertising networks, analytics services, or tracking technologies operated by the developer.

Data Security

User data is stored locally on user devices. Users are responsible for maintaining the security of their devices and backups.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time.
